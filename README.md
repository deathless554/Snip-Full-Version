## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Quick Capture** — Easily select a region of your screen to capture using your mouse.
- **Voice and Text Notes** — Add voice notes or text annotations directly onto your screenshots.
- **Drawing Tools** — Utilize brushes of various thicknesses and colors to enhance your images.
- **Flexible Saving Options** — Save your creations in BMP, PNG, JPEG, or GIF formats.
